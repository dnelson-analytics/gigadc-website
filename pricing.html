Pricing FAQ

What counts as capacity under management?

The Building Ready megawatts of the buildings in your plan, delivered or planned, before the net supply reserve.

Can I move between plans?

Yes. Upgrade whenever you add regions; your model and history carry over.

What data do I need to start?

A demand forecast by region and month, and a delivery schedule with a date for each milestone. Flat files are enough.

Is there a free trial?

The Pilot plan is the trial: one region, month to month, cancel any time.

Not sure which plan fits?

Tell us your regions and megawatts and we will size it with you.
