About

We build the capacity AI runs on, and the software that keeps it on schedule.

GigaDC Builders started as a data center developer. Delivering megawatts to a forecast taught us that the hard part is seeing, years ahead, where supply and demand will part ways.

What we believe

Three ideas shape the platform.

One axis for supply and demand

Forecast load and construction schedules belong on the same calendar and the same hierarchy, or the gap stays invisible.

Sellable power is net power

Gross megawatts overstate what you can commit. We count net of network gear and reserves, and show the factor.

Milestones, not guesses

Each building passes Facility Ready, Infrastructure Ready and Building Ready. Slips are visible the moment they happen.
