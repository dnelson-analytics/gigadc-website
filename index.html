The problem

Demand is a curve. Supply arrives in steps.

Forecasts drift away from construction

Demand lives in planning spreadsheets. Delivery lives in project schedules. Nobody sees the two on one axis.

Gaps appear late

A slipped milestone at one campus quietly turns a region from surplus into shortfall, and you learn about it at contract time.

Power is not the whole MW

Gross capacity is not sellable capacity. Network gear and reserves take their share, and your numbers should show it.

The platform

One model from region to building.

Demand

Forecast demand by region and month

Bring contracted and expected load into one forecast, in megawatts, from 2012 through 2040.

Supply

Supply built from real deliveries

Cumulative delivered capacity, net of the share reserved for network gear, rolled up from each building's Building Ready date.

Gap

Surplus and shortfall at a glance

Net supply minus demand over time, by Area, Region, Metro, Campus or Data Center, so you know where to build next.

Delivery

Milestone tracking per building

Facility Ready, Infrastructure Ready and Building Ready for every building. Delivered or planned relative to any as-of date.

  • Area to Region to Metro to Campus to Data Center hierarchy
  • Row-level security by territory
  • Semi-additive, end-of-period measures
  • Source-controlled reporting
